## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales alongside a rough stock list. She needs to understand how much the stall is earning, whether sales are growing, which days and products are performing well, and what stock to replenish. A concise analysis of sales patterns, weekday-versus-weekend performance, and reorder thresholds can help her make practical decisions about where to focus and how to use limited working capital.

## How to run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/seenoinaurori/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top three findings

1. Total sales over 14 days were **KES 46,450**, increasing from **KES 22,050** in week 1 to **KES 24,400** in week 2, a **10.7%** week-on-week increase.
2. Weekend average sales were **KES 5,875 per day**, compared with **KES 2,295** on weekdays, which is **156.0% higher**.
3. **Tomatoes (18 kg)** and **Sukuma (9 kg)** were at or below their reorder levels and should be considered for restocking.
